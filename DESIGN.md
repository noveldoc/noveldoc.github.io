# Novel Document Reader Website Design

## Direction

The site borrows the information hierarchy of a desktop software marketplace without copying any store's chrome. A compact product masthead introduces the icon, name, platform, price, and honest product promise; a wide, real application capture is the visual anchor.

## Palette

- Paper: `#ffffff`
- Canvas: `#f3f6fb`
- Ink: `#172033`
- Muted ink: `#5b6474`
- Reader blue: `#2f63d8`
- Reader blue dark: `#204aa8`
- Rule: `#d9e0ea`

## Typography

Figtree Variable is self-hosted and used across display and body text. Large type is reserved for the product name and section statements; policy pages use a narrower reading measure.

## Layout

Content follows a 1180px centered rail. The product masthead is compact and left aligned, followed by a full-width screenshot stage. Feature explanations alternate between prose and product evidence without nested cards. Policy and support content is presented as plain, scan-friendly sections.

## Components

- Buttons: 8px radius, strong verbs, visible focus, icon only when it adds meaning.
- Product icon: the extension's separately licensed blue document symbol.
- Screenshot switcher: segmented text buttons controlling one stable media frame.
- Fact rows: rules and aligned labels rather than promotional cards.
- Pricing: one framed purchase summary with explicit one-time billing and fulfillment.

## Motion

Only the screenshot crossfade and mobile navigation transition animate. Reduced-motion preferences remove both.

## Voice

Plain, specific, and calm. No invented social proof, urgency, superlatives, or claims of affiliation.
