A document workspace built around reading

Real extension interface. No conceptual mockups.

Reading controls where you need them

The extension changes the presentation of text you are allowed to view. It does not sell story content or bypass logins, subscriptions, paywalls, age gates, or other access controls.

Chapter-aware reading

Import a story's ordered chapter list, move between text blocks, and keep forewords or introductions in the author's original order.

Keyboard-first controls

Set shortcuts for previous and next text, chapter changes, temporary hiding, and a persistent pale-yellow reading highlight.

Built-in document templates

Use the included weekly update or a clean blank document, adjust the number of story paragraphs shown, and zoom from 50% to 200%.

Local-first document handling

Story text, uploaded documents, profile images, and reading settings stay in Chrome's local extension storage unless a payment-status request is made.

From story page to reading workspace

Set up once, then use your own shortcuts to keep reading.

  1. 1

    Install the extension

    Add Novel Document Reader from its public Chrome Web Store listing and pin it to the toolbar.

  2. 2

    Open a story details page

    Start from a supported Wattpad or Royal Road page that includes its chapter list. Compatible custom HTTPS fiction sites can be authorized separately.

  3. 3

    Launch and choose a chapter

    Open the extension. It reads the ordered chapter links and loads the first available chapter into the workspace.

  4. 4

    Set your reading controls

    Choose a template, paragraph count, highlight preference, zoom level, and keyboard shortcuts, then save the changes.

Clear boundaries, easy to verify

No story sales

Novel Document Reader is a presentation tool. Story rights and access remain with their authors, publishers, and source websites.

No payment-card storage

Stripe and ExtensionPay process checkout. The extension never receives complete card numbers or CVC values.

No behavioral advertising

The extension does not send document contents, story text, avatars, or reading preferences to the developer for advertising or analytics.
