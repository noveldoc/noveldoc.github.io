Refund and cancellation policy

Premium is a one-time digital purchase. There is no recurring subscription to cancel.

Effective: September 6, 2026Price: USD $4.99 one time

Refund requests

Refund requests are accepted within 14 calendar days of purchase. Include the purchase email, payment date, and a short description of the issue so the transaction can be located. Requests can be sent to noveldoc@outlook.com.

When a refund may be issued

A refund may be issued when Premium did not activate after reasonable troubleshooting, the purchase was duplicated, or the product was materially different from the description available at the time of purchase. Refund rights required by applicable law remain unaffected.

Processing time

Requests are normally reviewed within five business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Banks and card issuers may require additional time to post the credit.

Cancellation

Premium does not renew and has no recurring billing, so no cancellation action is required. Removing the extension does not automatically request a refund or delete records held by the payment processor.

Payment disputes

Please contact support first if a charge is unfamiliar or Premium access is missing. This allows the purchase to be checked and resolved promptly without limiting any rights available through the payment provider or card issuer.
